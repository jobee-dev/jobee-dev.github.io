# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 15, 2026

## Summary

Car Flip Tycoon does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All game progress is saved locally on your device only.

## Data We Collect

### None

The game runs entirely on-device. No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting tools, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party data collection are used.

### In-App Purchases

When you purchase Grandpa's Advice (monthly subscription or lifetime), the transaction is processed by **RevenueCat** and the platform's app store (Apple App Store or Google Play). We receive only a boolean flag indicating whether your purchase is active — no payment or personal information is shared with us.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at:  
<https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy>

### Local Storage

Game state (cars owned, money, upgrades, settings) is saved locally on your device using standard device storage (`AsyncStorage`). This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

- **RevenueCat** — processes in-app purchase verification. RevenueCat receives only transaction identifiers from the app store.
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — handle all payment processing under their own privacy policies.

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or social media services.

## Children's Privacy

This game is rated 4+ and does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your Rights

Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, delete, or export. Your entire game save resides on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if analytics are added in a future update), the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised and the changes will be described here.
