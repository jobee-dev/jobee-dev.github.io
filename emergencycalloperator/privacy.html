Privacy Policy — Emergency Call Operator

Last updated: 25 March 2026

Emergency Call Operator is developed and published by Jobee Development Ltd.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Emergency Call Operator mobile application ("the App").

1. Overview

Emergency Call Operator is a single-player simulation game. The App does not require users to create an account and does not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

However, some technical data may be collected automatically through integrated services in order to:

  • operate the app
  • analyse gameplay usage
  • manage subscriptions
  • send optional notifications

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Analytics Data

The App uses analytics tools to understand how the game is used and to improve gameplay.

This may include:

  • app launches
  • screen views
  • gameplay events (e.g. shifts completed)
  • crash reports
  • device type and operating system version

This information is anonymous and cannot identify you personally.

Analytics services used:

  • Firebase Analytics

2.2 Subscription and Purchase Data

The App offers optional premium subscriptions.

Subscription management is handled through:

  • Apple App Store
  • RevenueCat

RevenueCat may process information such as:

  • purchase status
  • subscription entitlement status
  • anonymous device identifiers

Emergency Call Operator does not directly process or store payment information. All payments are handled securely by Apple.

2.3 Notifications

If you grant permission, the App may send local notifications such as reminders that your shift quota has reset.

These notifications are optional and can be disabled at any time in your device settings.

The App does not send marketing notifications.

2.4 Gameplay Data

Gameplay progress is stored locally on your device and may include:

  • campaign progress
  • shift outcomes
  • upgrades purchased in-game
  • game settings

This data is used only to operate the game and is not shared externally.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected by the App is used to:

  • operate the game
  • improve gameplay balance and design
  • analyse feature usage
  • monitor stability and crashes
  • manage premium subscription access

We do not sell personal data and do not use your information for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

Emergency Call Operator uses several third-party services to provide functionality.

These services may collect limited technical data according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services used by the App include:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Apple App Store
  • RevenueCat

Please refer to their privacy policies for further details.

5. Data Storage

Most game data is stored locally on your device.

Analytics and subscription data may be transmitted securely to third-party services in order to operate the App.

All transmissions are encrypted using standard HTTPS connections.

6. Children's Privacy

Emergency Call Operator is not specifically directed at children under 13.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to remove the information.

7. Your Choices

You can control the data shared by the App by:

  • disabling notifications in device settings
  • disabling analytics at the device level (where supported)

You may also uninstall the App at any time to stop all data collection.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App or legal requirements.

Updates will be published within the App or on the App Store listing.

Emergency Call Operator collects only the minimal technical data required to operate the game and manage subscriptions. No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected or sold.
