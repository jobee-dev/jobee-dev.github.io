1. Overview

Hacker Sim: Cyber Ops (the "App") is a single‑player idle/progression game. We keep your game progress on your device and collect only the data described below to operate, improve, and monetize the App.

Unlike earlier versions, the App now displays advertising provided by Google AdMob, and — with your permission — uses an advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. This policy explains exactly what that means and how you can control it.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, camera, health, or calendar data. We do not operate a cloud‑save or user‑account system; your progress is stored locally on your device.

3. Advertising and Tracking

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your advertising identifier, advertising interactions, device information, and coarse (IP‑based) location to serve and measure ads.

Your choice (App Tracking Transparency). On iOS, before cross‑company tracking occurs we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

  • If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized using your advertising identifier.
  • If you decline, you will still see ads, but the App and AdMob cannot access your advertising identifier (IDFA) or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Ad selection and measurement may still use other data permitted by Apple and will respect your applicable consent and privacy settings; declining ATT by itself does not guarantee contextual‑only ads.

Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland. Where required, we present a Google‑certified consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) before serving ads, so you can grant or refuse consent for advertising and related analytics storage in line with GDPR.

Changing your mind. You can change these choices at any time:

  • In the App: Settings → Privacy → Manage Ad Privacy.
  • On iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android: Settings → Google → Ads.

Google's use of advertising data is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites/apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Operator Pass / ad‑free. Subscribers to our ad‑free entitlement are not shown ads and are not asked for tracking permission for advertising purposes.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to certain processing.

  • EEA/UK: GDPR rights, including the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to know, delete, and opt out of "sharing" of personal information for cross‑context behavioral advertising. You can exercise the opt‑out using the tracking/consent controls in Section 3. We do not sell personal information for money.

To exercise any right, contact support@jobee.dev. Because much of the data is pseudonymous and not linked to your identity, we may be unable to associate a request with a specific individual; we will ask for information needed to locate relevant data where possible.

Other controls:

  • Notifications: manage in your device Settings or in‑App settings.
  • Advertising: see Section 3.
  • Delete all local data: delete the App.

9. Children's Privacy

If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it. Advertising is configured for a general (non‑child‑directed) audience.
