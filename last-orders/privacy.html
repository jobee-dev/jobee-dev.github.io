Privacy Policy • Last Updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application "Last Orders" (the "App"), developed by Jobee Developments Ltd ("we," "us," or "our"), handles your information.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. No Personal Information Collected

We do not require or collect any personal information (such as your name, physical address, phone number, or email address) to download, install, or play Last Orders.

  • No User Accounts: The App does not require you to create an account or sign in with any custom credential system.
  • Local Storage: Your gameplay progress, custom settings, and pub design choices are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store this gameplay data on our own servers.

2. In-App Purchases & Licensing (RevenueCat)

Last Orders offers an optional one-time, non-consumable purchase (the "Full Licence"). To manage, validate, and restore this purchase, the App integrates the RevenueCat SDK, a service provided by RevenueCat, Inc.

Data Collected: When you interact with the in-app purchase system, RevenueCat collects:

  • Anonymized App User ID: A randomly generated identifier (UUID) used to link your device or Apple Account to your purchased entitlement. This identifier is anonymous and not linked to your real-world identity.
  • Purchase History & Receipts: Details of your transaction (such as transaction ID, purchase time, and product identifier) to verify your licence status.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Data: Information about your device (such as device model, operating system version, and app version) to ensure the purchase system operates correctly.

No Payment Processing: Neither we nor RevenueCat collect or process your sensitive payment information (such as credit card numbers or billing addresses). All payment processing is handled securely and exclusively by Apple via the iOS StoreKit framework.

For more information, please refer to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

3. Co-Op Multiplayer (Apple Game Center)

Last Orders features a 2-player cooperative multiplayer mode. This feature is powered by Apple's Game Center (GameKit).

  • Matchmaking & Invitations: When you host or join a co-op session, the App utilizes Apple's Game Center infrastructure to handle matchmaking, game invitations, and peer-to-peer data transport.
  • Game Center Data: Your Game Center alias, player identifier, and status are processed by Apple in accordance with the Apple Game Center terms and privacy policies.
  • No Server-Side Storage: We do not collect, store, or transmit your Game Center credentials, identity, or gameplay communications to any third-party servers. All multiplayer traffic is sent directly peer-to-peer or routed through Apple's GameKit framework.

4. No Advertising or Attribution Tracking

Last Orders is designed to be a clean, user-friendly simulation game.

  • No Ads: The App does not display third-party advertisements.
  • No Ad Tracking: We do not integrate any mobile measurement partners (MMPs), advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID tracking), or attribution services.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: We do not run third-party user behavior or product analytics services. The purchase-related analytics we access via RevenueCat are anonymous and used solely to verify purchase validity and diagnostic health.

5. Data Security & Retention

Any data processed by our third-party service providers (Apple and RevenueCat) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols. Because we do not run developer-controlled databases or servers, we do not host or store your personal data.

6. Data Deletion

  • Local Game Data: You can delete all local gameplay data by uninstalling the App from your device.
  • Purchase Telemetry: Because purchase telemetry processed by RevenueCat is tied to an anonymous, generated App User ID, we do not have a way to match this data to your real-world identity unless you provide your identifier. If you wish to request the deletion of any anonymous purchase history associated with your device, you may contact us using the contact details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Last Orders does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that we have accidentally collected personal information from a child, please contact us so we can delete it immediately.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on our website or within the App's settings, and by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
