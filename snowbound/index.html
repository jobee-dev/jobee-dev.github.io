Snowbound – Winter Plough

Satisfying snow clearing

The snow came down overnight and the whole town is waiting for you.

Snowbound is a cosy, oddly satisfying snow-ploughing game. Your plough drives itself, so just tap an arrow to choose your turn. Watch a deep, clean cut open behind your blade. Every street you clear lights up, and the town wakes up around you.
