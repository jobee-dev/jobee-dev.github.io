Snowbound support

Need a hand with the game?

Email support@jobee.dev. Put Snowbound in the subject and tell us what happened. If you can, include your iPhone model, iOS version and the game version. A screenshot can help us see the issue.

Restore a purchase

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. This restores eligible one-time unlocks. Consumable purchases such as diamond packs aren’t restored by this button.

If you were charged and the item is missing, email us with the item name, approximate purchase date and Apple order number if you have it. Leave out passwords and payment card details. You can request a refund from Apple.

Weekly leaderboard

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings and connect to the internet. The weekly leaderboard starts again each Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Privacy and notifications

Use Snowbound Settings to turn Analytics or reminders off. If Privacy Choices is shown, you can revisit your advertising settings there.
