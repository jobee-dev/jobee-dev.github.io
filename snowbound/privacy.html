Jobee Developments Ltd / Snowbound – Winter Plough

Privacy policy

Effective from 16 November 2026.

Jobee Developments Ltd is responsible for the information described in this policy. Snowbound has no separate sign-up or login. Your game progress and settings are stored on your device.

Advertising

Snowbound uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitial ads between shifts. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising privacy choices. Google may receive device identifiers, your IP address and approximate location, ad and app interactions, and crash and performance information to serve and measure ads and prevent abuse. Ad personalisation and cross-app tracking depend on your consent and your iOS tracking permission. When you decline, Snowbound only requests ads that Google's consent system permits.

Open Privacy Choices in Snowbound Settings when it is available to change your ad choices. Manage app tracking permissions in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Google privacy policy

Purchases and restores

Apple handles checkout and payment. RevenueCat receives purchase and transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier so we can validate purchases, restore eligible items, prevent fraud and understand sales. We do not give RevenueCat your name or email address. RevenueCat privacy policy

Weekly leaderboard

If you use Apple Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center identity, scores and rank to operate the leaderboard. What other players can see depends on your Game Center settings. We use Game Center information to run and display the leaderboard and do not share your Game Center identity with our advertising or analytics providers. Apple Game Center privacy notice

Optional game analytics

We use GameAnalytics only if you opt in. It receives a device identifier, approximate country inferred from your IP address, device and operating system details, gameplay events, purchase and ad events, and diagnostic information. We use this to understand how people play, improve Snowbound and identify problems. The SDK may receive Apple's advertising identifier if you have separately allowed tracking in iOS. You can turn Analytics off in Snowbound Settings to stop future analytics submissions. GameAnalytics privacy notice

Notifications, sharing and support

Optional reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in Snowbound or in iOS settings. When you share from Snowbound, iOS sends the selected content to the destination you choose. If you email support, we use your email address and any details you include to respond and resolve the issue.

Why we use the information

We use consent for optional analytics and for advertising where consent is required. We process purchase information to deliver what you buy and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. We rely on our legitimate interests to provide support, maintain the game, understand sales and protect the service from fraud where applicable. Providing analytics or advertising consent is optional.

Retention and international transfers

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose described above or as required by law. Our service providers apply their own retention periods to the information they handle. They may process information outside the UK and European Economic Area. Where required, transfers are covered by applicable safeguards such as adequacy decisions or contractual protections. Contact us for information about the safeguards that apply to your data.

Your choices and rights

You can contact us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your information where those rights apply. You can also withdraw consent and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email support@jobee.dev about a privacy request or complaint. In the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. In the EEA, you can contact your local data protection authority.
